# Craft Section Manager

The Website Settings tab now controls the second homepage video beside “Crafted, not produced”.

The administrator can:
- show or hide the craft video;
- upload a replacement MP4, WebM or MOV video;
- remove the current video without removing the text section;
- edit the small heading, title and description;
- edit the three numbered process steps.

This manager is separate from the Hero Manager.

The original local video remains available by default at `frontend/public/assets/atelier.mp4`.
